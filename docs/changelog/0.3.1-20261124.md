## 0.3.1

- **Snowcache Weasel storage support.** Weasels can sort items into Iron Chests and placed Sophisticated Backpacks, and gather Recipe Blueprint materials from them. Each weasel’s access setting follows its backpack when the backpack moves.
- **Refined Storage.** Grids and Crafting Grids default to take-only. Weasels deposit through Importers and collect from Exporters. Port priorities can be changed in their menus while carrying a weasel.
- **Create.** Weasels transfer items through Andesite and Brass Funnels connected to fixed Item Vaults. They respect funnel direction, filters, and redstone, with a configurable priority per port.
- **Weasel perching.** Fixed tamed weasels failing to perch on their owners on Fabric 1.21.11 and 26.3.
