## 0.2.0

- **Lumen Butterfly.** Added a tameable butterfly with an adult form, a caterpillar stage, and a spawn egg. Wild adults appear in groups of 2–4 in flower forests, plains, sunflower plains, and meadows. Configure natural spawning under `[spawns.lumen_butterfly]` with a default weight of 8.
- **Experience.** Wild adults absorb nearby experience orbs. When a player with adult followers kills a mob, its experience travels to the butterflies as particles and is shared among them. Right-click an owned butterfly to collect its stored experience. If it dies, its stored experience returns to its owner.
- **Color abilities.** Gold provides totem protection. Silver and red grant Speed and Strength on experience gain. Black, pink, and blue provide Night Vision, Regeneration, and Water Breathing, respectively. Blue also lets the swarm follow underwater. Green multiplies experience gains, brown speeds up nearby furnaces and brewing stands, and light blue adds Fortune and Looting. Matching colors can combine their effects.
- **Taming and breeding.** Tame a butterfly with the plant associated with its color. Adults can become ready to breed when their corresponding plants are within eight blocks. Offspring inherit both parents' lineages and are automatically tamed if both parents have the same owner.
- **Rainbow variant.** A butterfly whose lineage includes all nine colors gains an enchantment glint. While following, it can absorb damage to itself and the owner's other followers at one experience point per point of damage after armor and other reductions. Its balance can go down to −1,000 experience. Right-click it to repay the debt.
- **Following.** Up to seven butterflies can follow a player. Adults orbit their owner and turn to face their direction of flight. Following butterflies are immune to damage from their owner and can rejoin them from more than 32 blocks away or across dimensions.
- **Availability.** Lumen Butterflies are available on NeoForge 1.21.1, Forge 1.20.1, and Fabric 1.21.1, 1.21.11, and 26.3.
- **Mod icon.** Added a new icon featuring the creatures in the mod.
