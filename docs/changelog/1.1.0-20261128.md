## 1.1.0

- **Collector.** Meet a wandering barterer with six appearances tied to its habitat. Drop an item shown on one of its three tags to receive a surprise in return. Each completed offer renews independently. Data packs can customize the requests and rewards. The Collector has its own Xaero’s Minimap icon.
- **Field Guide.** With Patchouli installed, players receive an illustrated wildlife guide on their first login. Use a spyglass to record variants, view their portraits and discovery dates, and sort sightings by time or traits. The guide includes all 960 Kavat appearances. It is available on NeoForge 1.21.1, Forge 1.20.1, and Fabric 1.21.1 and 1.21.11. Fabric 1.21.11 uses Patchouli: Fabric Edition.
- **Guide entries.** Related Items now covers drops and other ways to obtain items from each creature. The Lumen Butterfly entry pairs all nine base colors with their vanilla plant icons and explains breeding readiness, the seven-follower limit, and how adult followers split experience from kills.
- **Air Bladders.** Shear an adult Bubble Salamander to collect a bladder in its color. The bladder grows back after five minutes. Adjoining bladders form a continuous membrane where players can swim, breathe, and see clearly. Rainbow bladders now change color seamlessly across adjoining blocks.
- **Long Feather Arrows.** A Condroc has a 50% chance to shed a flight feather each time it eats rotten flesh at a Vulture Post. Long Feather Arrows fly without gravity for two seconds, then fall with half the gravity of a regular arrow. Bows with Infinity still consume these arrows.
- **Snowcache Weasel Fur and work area.** Weasels occasionally shed fur while handling items in containers. Use it with a sample in your offhand to set a container’s item or creative tab category. The category survives emptying the container. Sneak with an adult weasel on your head to highlight its work point and eligible containers within 16 blocks.
- **Salvage Ray Molt.** A young Salvage Ray sheds a molt when it grows up. Apply it to armor or elytra in an anvil for one experience level to add single-use Void Protection and preserve the existing anvil penalty. When you are about to fall into the void, the coating makes a temporary platform that cracks before dispersing after five seconds. Hold Shift over coated gear to see its protection.
- **Wildlife and textures.** Updated creature sounds and added more Kavat idle calls. Velocipods heal when eating sugar cane, Lumen Butterflies when absorbing experience, and Salvage Rays and Snowcache Weasels when eating. Kavats heal when fed meat or left to rest. Adjusted Kavat spawn weights and Condroc habitats. Updated textures for Long Feather Arrows, Snowcache Weasel Fur, and Salvage Ray Molt.
