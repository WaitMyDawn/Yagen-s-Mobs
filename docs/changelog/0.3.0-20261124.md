## 0.3.0

- **Salvage Ray.** Added a creature that nests on the End's outer islands, with juvenile forms, five colors, and one-, two-, and three-tailed variants. It retrieves dropped items, catches items falling into the void, and rescues registered players from high falls.
- **Salvage Nest Box.** Stores recovered items and gives rays a home. Link boxes with a compass to set up passenger routes. Rays can also help rig rock-silk lines between islands beyond the launcher's usual range.
- **Snowcache Weasel.** Added a tameable creature that spawns in snowy biomes, with juvenile forms and four colors. Tamed adults sort nearby chests by creative tab, with separate controls for what they can take from or put into each chest. Adults perch on the player's head, and juveniles perch on the shoulder.
- **Recipe Blueprint.** With JEI installed, players can plan crafting jobs and send a tamed adult weasel to collect materials and craft at a workbench. The weasel can also gather ingredients and serving containers for Farmer's Delight cooking pot recipes.
- **Display fixes.** Fixed missing translations, hard-to-read titles, and missing spawn egg textures on newer Fabric versions. Click the title to rename a Salvage Nest Box or Vulture Post.
- **Xaero map support.** Added face icons for all eight creatures in Xaero's Minimap entity radar, including its overlay in Xaero's World Map.
