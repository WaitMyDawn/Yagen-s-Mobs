## 1.0.0

- **Kavats.** Added feline companions with three breeds, four ear shapes, five tail shapes and sixteen coats. Wild Kavats appear across the Overworld, Nether and End. Look for Spore Blossom Pink in lush caves, Magma Red in basalt deltas and Dragon Breath Purple on the central End island.
- **Genetics and incubation.** Collect ten Kavat Genetic Codes with extractors, or pair two recorded Genetic Imprints to breed for particular traits. The incubator opens after five minutes, and your Kavat uncurls on the tray and stretches. If a companion dies, revive it with the material matching its coat. It keeps its inherited traits and remaining imprints.
- **Breed abilities.** A Smeeta vanishes and draws enemies to a decoy. An Adarza can reflect damage back at its attacker. A following Vasca can spend its own health to save its owner from a fatal hit. Each breed has its own face on Xaero’s Minimap.
- **Kavat behavior.** Kavats pounce on distant combat targets, swim and dive with their owners, curl up beside a lit furnace or campfire, and roll on the ground to put out flames. Sneak-right-click with an empty hand to switch between following and staying. Following Kavats and Lumen Butterflies can rejoin their owners after long-distance teleports and dimension changes.
- **Pet commands and safety.** Bind **Disengage visible pets** in Controls to call off your own pets that are on screen and in clear sight. They keep their follow or stay orders. Tamed companions steer around hazardous blocks, while Lumen Butterflies can still visit cacti and wither roses safely.
- **Velocipod riding.** Riders shift their weight when boarding and dismounting, and can draw bows, use crossbows, throw tridents and eat while riding. A tamed Velocipod glides toward a safe landing after an airborne dismount and helps its owner attack while unridden.
- **Wildlife and traversal.** Desert Skates and savanna Condrocs are easier to encounter, and Rock Hangers can find more suitable cave ceilings. Fixed grabbing Rock Silk from above a ledge. Waiting Lumen Butterflies flap as they land, then gently move their wings and antennae at rest.
